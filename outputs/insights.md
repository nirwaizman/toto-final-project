## Key Business Insights

- **🚨 City Hotel cancellations are a critical revenue risk.**
  City Hotel posts a 41.8% cancellation rate vs. 28.1% for Resort Hotel — nearly 50% higher.
  Over 4 in 10 city hotel bookings never materialise. Revenue management teams should
  implement aggressive overbooking buffers and non-refundable deposit policies specifically
  for City Hotel to protect realised revenue.

- **💰 City Hotel commands a price premium but pays for it in uncertainty.**
  At €105.51 ADR vs. €95.48 for Resort Hotel (~10% premium), City Hotel generates higher
  per-night yield — but the elevated cancellation rate erodes that advantage. Net revenue
  per available room (RevPAR) may be closer between the two hotels than headline ADR
  suggests. A combined yield + cancellation analysis is recommended before setting rate strategy.

- **🌍 Portugal is the dominant demand source — but concentration is a risk.**
  With 47,784 bookings, Portuguese guests represent a disproportionate share of total demand,
  consistent with the hotels being Portugal-based. While domestic demand provides a stable
  base, over-reliance on a single origin market creates vulnerability to local economic
  downturns or travel disruptions. Diversification into GBR and FRA feeder markets should
  be a strategic priority.

- **🇬🇧🇫🇷 UK and France are the most valuable international feeder markets.**
  GBR (12,100 bookings) and FRA (10,310 bookings) are the top two international origins,
  followed by ESP (8,263) and DEU (7,284). Marketing investment in these four Western
  European countries offers the highest ROI for international demand generation. Targeted
  campaigns in these markets — especially via OTA-sponsored placements — should be prioritised.

- **📣 OTAs control nearly half of all bookings — commission costs are substantial.**
  Online Travel Agencies account for 47.9% of bookings (56,318), and combined with Offline
  TA/TO (20.3%), intermediaries control ~68% of total volume. At typical OTA commission
  rates of 15–20%, this represents a significant cost drag on margins. Growing the Direct
  channel (currently only 10.5%) by even 5 percentage points could meaningfully improve
  net revenue per booking.

- **🏢 Corporate segment is small but strategically high-value.**
  Corporate bookings represent just 4.3% of volume but typically carry lower cancellation
  rates and guaranteed-night contracts. Investing in corporate sales and loyalty programmes
  could improve booking certainty and reduce dependence on high-cancellation OTA channels.

- **⏱️ A 104.6-day average lead time enables proactive revenue management.**
  Guests book roughly 3.5 months in advance on average, providing a meaningful forecasting
  window. This lead time should be leveraged to implement dynamic early-bird pricing tiers,
  trigger targeted re-engagement campaigns for bookings at high cancellation risk (e.g.,
  long lead time + OTA channel + no deposit), and optimise inventory release schedules.

- **📊 Overall 37.3% cancellation rate demands a predictive cancellation model.**
  More than 1 in 3 bookings is cancelled. A machine learning model trained to predict
  cancellation probability at booking time would allow the revenue team to apply differential
  policies — stricter deposit requirements or overbooking allowances — for high-risk bookings,
  directly protecting revenue without alienating low-risk guests.

## ⚠️ Data Leakage Warning for Modelling Teams

The following columns are **post-outcome labels** and must **NEVER** be used as model features
in any cancellation prediction model. They are known only after the booking outcome is resolved
and would cause severe data leakage:

| Column | Reason |
|---|---|
| `reservation_status` | Directly encodes the target outcome (Canceled / Check-Out / No-Show) |
| `reservation_status_date` | The date the status was set — only exists post-outcome |
